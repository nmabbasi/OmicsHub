04 · WORK LIKE A SCIENTIST

Use each tutorial as a small reproducible practice loop.

Progress comes from running a workflow, checking what it produced, and recording why your choices were appropriate—not merely reading every page in order.

  1. LearnRead the objective and prerequisites before opening a tool.
  2. RunUse a small public or practice dataset to execute the workflow.
  3. RecordSave parameters, versions, outputs, and important decisions.
  4. ReflectUse the knowledge check to explain what could change the result.

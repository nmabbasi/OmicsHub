KNOW WHAT YOU ARE OPENING

Four formats.
Different kinds of learning.

A format tells you what a lesson offers, not whether an analysis is validated.

01 Hands-on lab
Follow a focused exercise and inspect its outputs.
02 Conceptual guide
Understand methods, assumptions, and interpretation.
03 Advanced template
Prepare compatible inputs, adapt the code, and check it.
04 Reference
Look up formats, commands, and troubleshooting checks.

Not every biological workflow has been run end to end. Use each lesson's setup notes, prerequisites, and outputs to judge its scope, and verify relevant steps in your environment.

Example: what the PBMC lab covers

The scRNA-seq Basics lab starts with a public PBMC 3k count matrix and offers Seurat or Scanpy examples for QC, normalization, clustering, and UMAP. It is a focused matrix-to-clustering exercise, not a raw-read processing pipeline or a clinical validation workflow.

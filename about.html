About This Resource

Practical bioinformatics guides built by researchers, written for everyday use in real laboratory and HPC environments.

Reviewed: August 2026

Our Purpose

Learning computational biology often requires bridging a significant gap between reading methods sections in papers and actually executing those pipelines at the command line. This platform was built specifically to address that challenge. Every tutorial is structured around hands-on, step-by-step guidance so you can directly apply these tools to your own genomic datasets from day one.

The instructions here are tested in real HPC environments and updated to reflect current software versions. Whether you are running your first Bash command or assembling a production-ready scRNA-seq pipeline, the goal is the same: remove friction between you and the analysis.

About the Author

The Omics Hub is created and maintained by Nasir Mahmood Abbasi, PhD, a Bioinformatician with extensive expertise in computational biology, data analysis, and scalable workflow development. The tutorials share the exact pipelines, configuration files, and best practices used in active, high-level academic research, avoiding theoretical examples in favor of real-world applicability.

With a deep technical background in analyzing complex biological datasets - including single-cell RNA-seq, bulk transcriptomics, metagenomics, and multi-omics integration - the focus of this hub is to enforce reproducibility, clarity, and scientific rigor. Feedback and suggestions for new topics are always welcome through the Contact page.

Editorial Standards & Updates

Tutorials are designed to make analytical choices explicit: the input data, software environment, important parameters, expected outputs, and interpretation limits should be visible to the learner. Guidance is reviewed when major software behavior, recommended workflows, or evidence changes.

Examples are educational and should be adapted to the dataset, species, tissue, sequencing chemistry, institutional policy, and research question at hand. Where an analysis is sensitive to design choices, the tutorial explains the assumption rather than presenting a fixed threshold or default as universally correct.

If you spot an error, a broken reference, or a result that needs clarification, please report it through the Contact page. Corrections are prioritized and the relevant tutorial review date is updated when a material change is made.

Professional Transparency

The Omics Hub is independently maintained by Nasir Mahmood Abbasi, PhD. Professional profiles and code are available on LinkedIn and GitHub. Consulting availability, scope, and pricing are described separately on the Services page.

What You Will Learn

  • Shell & Bash Scripting: Essential Unix/Linux commands for file management, text processing, and pipeline automation.
  • High-Performance Computing (HPC): Connecting to clusters, writing Slurm job scripts, and managing resources efficiently.
  • Conda & Mamba: Creating isolated, reproducible software environments for bioinformatics tools.
  • Single-cell RNA-seq Analysis: End-to-end workflows from raw FASTQ files to cell clustering and biological interpretation.
  • Bioinformatics Fundamentals: Core concepts in genomics, data formats, and workflow design for reproducible science.

Who These Tutorials Are For

These tutorials are written for graduate students, postdoctoral researchers, and lab staff who are building computational skills alongside wet-lab work. No prior programming background is assumed for the introductory modules. Advanced tutorials assume familiarity with basic Unix commands and the concepts covered in earlier guides in the series.

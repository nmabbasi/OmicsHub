About Bioinformatics Workflow Hub

Welcome to Bioinformatics Workflow Hub. We provide structured, practical tutorials focused on shell scripting, high-performance computing (HPC), and single-cell RNA-seq analysis. Our goal is to make complex computational workflows accessible and reproducible for researchers at all levels.

Our Purpose

Learning computational biology often requires bridging a significant gap between theoretical papers and the actual command-line execution of tools. This platform was built to address that exact challenge. We focus heavily on hands-on, step-by-step methodology so you can directly apply these tools to your own genomic datasets.

Our guides are designed to be immediately useful. Whether you are running your first Bash script or clustering a large scRNA-seq dataset, the instructions here are tested and ready for production use.

About the Author

Bioinformatics Workflow Hub is maintained by bioinformaticians with direct experience in academic research and data analysis. We built this resource to share the exact pipelines and best practices we use daily, fostering better reproducibility and collaboration in the scientific community.

All tutorials are regularly reviewed to ensure they work with current software versions. We invite you to explore the tutorials, apply the code to your own projects, and reach out if you have any feedback or suggestions for new topics.

What You'll Learn

  • Bioinformatics Fundamentals: Core concepts and principles of computational biology.
  • Shell Commands: Essential Unix/Linux commands for data manipulation and automation.
  • High-Performance Computing (HPC): Tutorials on utilizing clusters for large-scale analyses.
  • Conda & Mamba: Efficient package and environment management for reproducible research.
  • Single-cell RNA-seq: In-depth guides from raw data processing to biological interpretation.

Join the Bioinformatics Workflow Hub community today and embark on a transformative learning experience. Whether you're a student, a researcher, or a professional looking to upskill, we're here to support your growth in the exciting world of bioinformatics.

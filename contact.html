Send a Message

Ask a question about a tutorial, report a broken command, suggest a new guide, or reach out about a collaboration or data request.

For privacy and research safety, do not upload participant-identifying data, protected health information, passwords, private keys, or restricted research data.

Your name, email, message, and any attachment are transmitted through FormSubmit to contact@theomicshub.com solely so we can respond. Do not include confidential or participant-identifying data. See the Privacy Policy for data retention and deletion details.

What to Expect After You Write

Every message is reviewed personally and answered within 24 to 48 business hours. No automated responses or generic acknowledgements are sent.

If you are asking about a bioinformatics workflow or troubleshooting step, the most helpful details to include are the tutorial title or section, the software version and environment (such as R, Python, Conda, or Slurm), the exact error message, and what you expected to happen.

If you are reporting a typo, outdated flag, or broken reference link in a tutorial, include the page URL or title so it can be verified and corrected quickly.

Common Reasons Readers Get in Touch

This contact channel supports learners, researchers, and visitors across the platform:

  • 1. Tutorial questions and troubleshooting: Clarifications on single-cell RNA-seq, bulk transcriptomics, metagenomics, Conda/Mamba, or HPC workflow steps covered on the site.
  • 2. Corrections and software updates: Reporting a broken link, deprecated package flag, or command whose behavior has changed in a newer release.
  • 3. New tutorial suggestions: Proposing computational biology methods, diagnostic checks, or reproducible workflow topics that would help the wider community.
  • 4. Academic collaboration and mentoring: Discussing educational workshops, open teaching materials, or structured bioinformatics guidance.
  • 5. Privacy and data requests: Requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of past messages or newsletter subscriptions under GDPR or CCPA.

Scope & Safety Guidelines

The Omics Hub is an independent educational publication. Correspondence through this page does not provide medical advice, clinical interpretation of patient samples, or institutional compliance sign-off.

Never send patient-identifiable information, protected health information (PHI), raw clinical records, credentials, or restricted institutional data by email or form attachment. Use anonymized or minimal synthetic examples when describing an error.

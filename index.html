Learn with context, not just commands

Use the explanations, setup notes, and references to understand why a method fits your question and what you need to check yourself.

Understand the data before choosing a cutoff.

Use diagnostic plots and biological context to justify your choices, rather than applying the same threshold to every dataset.

Learning Example: PBMC 3k QC and Clustering

The scRNA-seq Basics lab starts with a public PBMC 3k count matrix and presents Seurat and Scanpy examples for QC, normalization, clustering, and UMAP. It explains why gene-count and mitochondrial metrics need biological context. This is a focused matrix-to-clustering exercise, not a raw-read processing pipeline or a clinical validation workflow.

Explore the PBMC QC-to-clustering lab →

Check examples in your environment

Where provided, use setup notes, expected outputs, and diagnostic checks to assess an example. Not every biological workflow has been run end to end; verify the relevant steps in your environment before applying them to research data.

Meet the Author: Nasir Mahmood Abbasi, PhD

Computational Biologist & Data Scientist

Welcome to The Omics Hub. I hold a PhD in Cancer Biology and specialise in single-cell multi-omics, cancer genomics, and reproducible computational workflows. I built this platform to help researchers move from biological questions to practical, command-line analysis.

The lessons focus on practical research decisions, from single-cell integration to workflows on a cluster. The aim is to explain the analysis so you can adapt examples responsibly.
